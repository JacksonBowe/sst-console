# App domain

- `app.ts`, `app.stage.ts`, `resource.ts`: App, Stage, and Resource operations, including disconnect cleanup.
- `app.stage.ts`: Stage upsert also maintains its parent App without reconciling Resources.
- `projection.ts`: reconciles complete Resource observations into App projections.
- `ownership.ts`: Stage ownership conflict handling.
- `events.ts`: Stage projection removal requests and removed events.
- `app.dynamo.ts`, `schema.ts`, `errors.ts`: App persistence, contracts, and errors.
