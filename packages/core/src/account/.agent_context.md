# Account domain

- `account.ts`: Account CRUD and listing; sole owner of Account persistence operations.
- `connector.ts`: assumed-role verification and connector callbacks.
- `discovery.ts`: bootstrap/state-object discovery and remote-access disconnect handling.
- `sync.ts`: synchronization orchestration and projection persistence.
- `management.ts`: sync-policy management views and apply operation.
- `projection.ts`: removes account-owned projections after disconnect.
- `inspect.ts`, `state.ts`: account topology and safe SST state inspection.
- `schema.ts`, `errors.ts`, `ownership.ts`: shared Account contracts/helpers.
